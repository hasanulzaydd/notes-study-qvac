Cell bio lecture, ch 6-7

- mitochondria = "powerhouse" thing, makes ATP, has its own DNA (weird, separate
  from nucleus). prof mentioned endosymbiotic theory - basically an old bacteria
  got swallowed by a bigger cell way back and they just... stayed. explains the
  separate DNA + double membrane.

- chloroplasts only in plants, do photosynthesis. light + water + CO2 -> sugar +
  oxygen. remember the reaction has 6 of CO2 and 6 of water, makes glucose
  (C6H12O6) and 6 O2. happens inside the chloroplast obviously.

- DNA replication: NOT copied all at once from scratch — semi conservative,
  so each new strand pairs with ONE old strand + one new one. polymerase does
  this but reads in opposite direction from how it writes (3'->5' read, 5'->3'
  write, always confuses me, need to just memorize it)

- osmosis = water moving across membrane, always toward MORE solute (higher
  concentration side pulls water in) until it evens out. don't mix up with
  diffusion which is more general (any particle, not just water)
